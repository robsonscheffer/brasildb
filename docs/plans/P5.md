# P5: one place picker and one top bar for the whole site

Goal: a reader picks their place (city, then zona and seção) once, with one control, and every
page uses it: the home walk, the count page (`apuracao.html`) and `como.html`. No page keeps its
own form. The count page gets linked from the home only after this lands.

## Facts this plan builds on (checked 2026-10-31)

Where the place is picked today:
- U1 Home, "seu dia" (fold 03): free-text city search, then the hour, then a hidden "Ver a sua
  urna" link that opens free-text zona and seção fields. A wrong number gives "Ainda não temos".
- U2 Home, fold 05: says "digite zona e seção para ver a sua" with no field there (PR #59 added
  a second form; closed in favour of this plan).
- U3 Home, card: "Escolher cidade" scrolls back to U1.
- U4 Count page: four dropdowns (Estado, Cidade, Zona, Seção), Brasil by default; the city box
  stays disabled until a state is picked. The map's city tooltip has "Ver a apuração da cidade".
- U5 URLs: the home uses `cidade, zona, secao, hora`; the count page uses `uf, cidade, zona,
  secao`. A place picked on one page does not reach the other, and nothing links them.

Data:
- Zona and seção alone do not identify a section: 86.194 pairs repeat across states, one pair
  in up to 23 states. State, zona and seção always do (0 repeats in 499.207 sections). 1.639 of
  the 2.641 zonas cover more than one city. The título and the e-Título print city, state, zona
  and seção together, so the city is always at hand.
- A list of every city's zonas and section ranges is 653 KB raw for 5.717 cities; the median
  city is 73 bytes gzip, the largest (São Paulo) 5,9 KB gzip.
- Generated site data is in git and served by GitHub Pages: the home's 6.223 section files are
  53 MB; the count page's 5.715 city files are 33 MB, not committed yet. The pack is 74 MB.

## Decisions

- D1 One place: `{cidade, zona, secao}`, plus `hora` on the home. One URL contract on every
  page: `cidade`, `zona`, `secao`, `hora`. The count page adds `ver` (`brasil`, `uf`, `cidade`,
  `zona`) for a level above the place. Old count links with `uf=` keep working: read, then
  rewritten.
- D2 One picker, in one shared file (`docs/site/lugar.js` and its CSS): type a city, pick
  "Três Cachoeiras, RS" (abroad, "Cidade, País"); then zona and seção are lists filled from
  the data, so a section we do not have cannot be picked. Seção is optional. It shows inline in
  fold 03 and as a sheet everywhere else. The hour stays a home step after the city.
- D3 One top bar on all three pages: the site name, Início · A apuração · Como contamos, and the
  place chip: "Três Cachoeiras, RS · zona 0085 · seção 0008 · trocar", or "Escolha o seu lugar"
  before a pick. The chip opens the sheet.
- D4 A fold that needs more of the place says what it needs and opens the sheet: fold 05 says
  "Esta é a urna típica da cidade. Escolher a sua seção". The card's "Escolher cidade" opens
  the sheet. No fold holds its own fields.
- D5 The count page's scale is the place's path, as tabs: Brasil › RS › Três Cachoeiras › zona
  0085 › seção 0008. Brasil is the view before a pick. Picking a city on the map views it
  without changing the place, and the path shows "voltar para Três Cachoeiras".
- D6 Internal links carry the place in the URL (home ↔ count page ↔ como). No storage.
- D7 Data: `docs/site/data/lugares/<UF>.json`, `{city: {zona: [section or [first, last]]}}`,
  with aggregated sections listed and mapped to their principal as on the home. Both pages read
  it after a city pick.
- D8 Storage: commit the count page's data to git like the home's; round 2 is final, so
  rebuilds are rare. Before a new round or a new dataset, move all generated data to object
  storage behind a data host, in its own plan.

## Open questions

- Q1 D5: should picking a city on the count page's map stay view-only, or replace the place?
  The plan says view-only, so "sua seção" never points at a city the reader did not choose.
- Q2 Does the top bar's chip show the hour on the home ("· 14h às 15h")? The plan says no: the
  hour is a home answer, not part of the place.
