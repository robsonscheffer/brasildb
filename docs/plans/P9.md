# P9: campaign money, donors' companies, public contracts and sanctions

Goal: for each candidate, the lake shows where the campaign money came from (party funds,
own money, individual donors and the economic sectors of the companies those donors own), what
it was spent on, the candidate's declared assets over time, and whether donor or supplier
companies hold public contracts or sanctions. Needs P6 (`id_pessoa`, municipality key).

## Facts this plan builds on (checked 2026-11-01 unless marked)

- F1 TSE `prestacao_de_contas_eleitorais_candidatos_<ano>.zip`: receipts and expenses. In 2026,
  all 102,790 individual-donor rows show the donor CPF with 11 digits. Donor municipality is
  empty for individuals (research note a6).
- F2 2026 receipts by origin, all filing types: party funds R$ 5,680M, individuals R$ 787M,
  own money R$ 192M, other candidates R$ 133M. Party funds are about 83%.
- F3 2026 accounts are not final: deadlines 3 Nov (round 1) and 14 Nov (round 2), from news
  summaries; not checked on the TSE site.
- F4 Receita CNPJ open data: partners (QSA) show name and the 6 middle CPF digits (research
  notes a6, a7; layout PDF read by the agent, not re-checked).
- F5 PNCP API `api/consulta/v1/contratos?cnpjOrgao=`: Blumenau city hall, 2025, 2,269
  contracts. There is no municipality filter; query by each public body's CNPJ.
- F6 CGU CEIS download (`download-de-dados/ceis/<yyyymmdd>`) works and has
  `CPF OU CNPJ DO SANCIONADO`, no city column. IBAMA fines carry full CPF/CNPJ (note a9).
- F7 Candidate assets (`bens_candidato_<ano>`) and expenses with supplier CNPJ and CNAE
  (note a8, not re-run).

## Decisions (2026-11-01)

- D1 Load 2022 and 2024 first; 2026 after the round 2 deadline.
- D2 Donor to partner match on (normalized name, CPF digits 4 to 9). Only exact matches on
  both count; report the match rate per year.
- D3 Person-level match results are personal data (LGPD). They stay in a private dataset;
  public tables and the site show counts and totals per candidate, party, sector and
  municipality.
- D4 Pilot: SC statewide and Blumenau for the donor and contract match; Três Cachoeiras is a
  hand check, too small to show a pattern.

## Tasks

- T1 TSE candidates, receipts, expenses, assets for 2022, 2024: loaders and `id_pessoa`.
- T2 Receita CNPJ: companies, establishments (CNAE, municipality), partners.
- T3 Donor to partner match (D2), private table plus public aggregates by sector.
- T4 PNCP contracts for every municipal and state body in SC and RS.
- T5 CEIS, CNEP and IBAMA lists; exact joins on CPF/CNPJ to donors and suppliers.
- T6 Views: money by origin per candidate; donor sector per candidate; suppliers and donors
  with contracts or sanctions (counts only in public views).

## Done when

- Every 2022 and 2024 candidate in SC and RS has receipts by origin that add up to the TSE total.
- The match rate for D2 is known and written in this plan.
