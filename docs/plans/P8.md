# P8: below the municipality, through voting places and census tracts

Goal: each voting place gets the profile of its voters (TSE) and of the census tract it stands
in (IBGE 2022), so large cities can be read place by place. Needs P6 T4 (voting places).

## Facts this plan builds on (checked 2026-11-01 unless marked)

- F1 Voting places have coordinates (P6 F4). Blumenau 60 places, 757 sections; Três
  Cachoeiras 10 places, 28 sections.
- F2 IBGE tract results, folder `Censos/Censo_Demografico_2022/Agregados_por_Setores_Censitarios/
  Agregados_por_Setor_csv/`: literacy, basic, household characteristics 1 to 3, race, demography,
  parenthood, deaths, indigenous, quilombola. No income file, and the data dictionary has no
  "rendimento" field.
- F3 Tract polygons: IBGE 2022 shapefiles per state. Blumenau 643 tracts, Três Cachoeiras 34
  (research notes a1, a5, not re-run).
- F4 TSE voter profile per section, 2026 (`perfil_eleitor_secao_2026_<UF>.zip`: age, sex,
  schooling per section; research note a5, not re-run).

## Decisions (2026-11-01)

- D1 Join: point in polygon, voting place to tract. Voronoi cells around places only as a
  check of how much the result moves.
- D2 Claims stay at place level: "places where X is higher voted more for Y". A place's voters
  are not the tract's residents.
- D3 Três Cachoeiras is a fixture and a map, not a statistical case.
- D4 Income below the municipality waits for P10 (night lights).

## Tasks

- T1 `br_tse_eleicoes.perfil_eleitor_secao` loader and test (pilot sections).
- T2 `br_ibge_censo_2022.setor_*` loaders for the files in F2, SC and RS first.
- T3 Tract polygons to GeoParquet; DuckDB spatial join in `sql/views/70_local_setor.sql`:
  `main.local_setor(ano, id_municipio, zona, id_local_votacao, id_setor)`.
- T4 `main.local_social`: place, votes per candidate, voter profile, tract profile.
