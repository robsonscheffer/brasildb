# P7: social data per municipality, every municipality

Goal: for each of the 5,570 municipalities, the lake holds income, employment, federal cash
benefits, social assistance spending and formal jobs, all on `id_municipio`. A view
`main.municipio_social` puts them next to the vote share per candidate, so one query answers
"municipalities with more X voted more for Y". Needs P6 (the SIAFI code).

Why every municipality and not the two pilot cities: with two data points any pattern is
chance. The pilot cities are the test fixtures.

## Facts this plan builds on (checked 2026-11-01 unless marked)

Income and work, Census 2022 (one API call per table for all municipalities):
- F1 SIDRA 10295: mean and median household income per capita. Blumenau R$ 2,633.68 /
  1,855.00; Três Cachoeiras R$ 1,632.48 / 1,300.00.
- F2 SIDRA 10268: employment level, age 10+. Blumenau 63.55%, Três Cachoeiras 54.16%.
- F3 No official unemployment rate exists per municipality; PNAD Contínua is state level.

Federal cash benefits, Portal da Transparência bulk files (no key), monthly:
- F4 Bolsa Família `download-de-dados/novo-bolsa-familia/<yyyymm>`: 330 MB zip, 18.7M rows for
  2026-09. One row per payment: `MÊS COMPETÊNCIA`, `MÊS REFERÊNCIA`, `UF`,
  `CÓDIGO MUNICÍPIO SIAFI`, `NOME MUNICÍPIO`, `CPF FAVORECIDO` (masked), `NIS FAVORECIDO`,
  `NOME FAVORECIDO`, `VALOR PARCELA` (comma decimal). A payment month can pay an earlier
  reference month.
- F5 BPC `download-de-dados/bpc/<yyyymm>`: 190 MB zip, 6.6M rows for 2026-09. Same city and
  month columns, beneficiary and legal representative (NIS, CPF, name; beneficiary blank
  under 16), `NÚMERO BENEFÍCIO`, `BENEFÍCIO CONCEDIDO JUDICIALMENTE`, `VALOR PARCELA`. No
  column for old age vs disability.
- F6 2026-09 pilot totals: Bolsa Família Blumenau 6,371 payments (6,335 for September),
  about R$ 4.21M; Três Cachoeiras 228, R$ 151,242. BPC Blumenau 3,440, R$ 5,576,455 (903 by
  court, 1,090 under 16); Três Cachoeiras 224, R$ 363,514.
- F7 The Portal API (`api.portaldatransparencia.gov.br`, free key) has
  `novo-bolsa-familia-por-municipio` and `bpc-por-municipio` with `codigoIbge`, `mesAno`,
  returning `valor` and `quantidadeBeneficiados`. Schema read from `/v3/api-docs`; not called
  yet (no key).

Spending and jobs:
- F8 SICONFI DCA (`apidatalake.tesouro.gov.br/ords/siconfi/tt/dca`, `id_ente` = IBGE code):
  function "08 - Assistência Social", Blumenau 2025 paid R$ 72,703,297.57.
- F9 Novo CAGED and RAIS per municipality (research note a3, not re-run): Blumenau formal jobs
  149,562 (Dec 2024), CAGED net +574 for Sep 2025 to Aug 2026.

## Decisions (2026-11-01)

- D1 Store aggregates only: one row per municipality, month and program. Names, CPF and NIS
  are read in memory and dropped.
- D2 Count both views of a month: `parcelas` and `valor` by payment month, plus
  `parcelas_atrasadas` (reference month earlier than payment month).
- D3 Bulk files are the default source: no key, and they give counts the API lacks (court
  grants, under 16, distinct NIS, late payments). The API, once the key exists, is a check of
  the monthly totals.
- D4 Months loaded: January 2022 to the latest, so P6's 2022 and 2026 elections both have the
  twelve months before them.
- D5 Unemployment proxy: Census employment level, with CAGED net jobs for the trend.

- `main.municipio_social`: one row per (ano, turno, id_municipio, cargo, candidate) with vote
  share and the latest social values before the election date.

## Done when
