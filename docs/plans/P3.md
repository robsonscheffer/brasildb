# P3: "O Brasil votando" v2, the walk

Goal: a new front page that walks a voter from one vote to the whole country over about ten
screens, asks where they voted, shows the map with their town ringed, runs the close and the
counting night, and ends on their own card: "você foi um dos 127.854.844". The v1 map stays as
the explore page. No claim about the result; no side; no fraud talk.

Why a new page and not more of v1: v1, the urna's diary prototype (PR #17, closed) and three
format sketches (branch `feat/site-sketches`) all showed an aggregate, or one anonymous urna, in
a single fold. Nobody on the page was the reader. The data stays; the page now builds the picture
up, screen by screen, so the main element lands, and it turns personal half way through.

Facts this plan builds on (checked 2026-10-29):
- Round 2 lake (`data/lake`, reparsed, nested `.jez` read): 497,896 sections with a log,
  127,854,844 `voto_computado` rows. 497,887 sections match the TSE turnout; 9 are off
  (`sql/checks/comparecimento.sql`), 1,352 TSE-aggregated sections have no log.
- `main.secao_dia`: one row per section with abertura, primeiro_voto, ultimo_voto, encerramento,
  votos, habilitados, biometricos, tentativas_digital, justificativas, quedas, min_bateria,
  contingencia, modelo. Times are the urna's clock; `main.fuso_municipio` gives Brasília.
- `data/raw/cs/<uf>-p003221-cs.json` (54 files, `SHA256SUMS`): the TSE's arrival time of each
  section's result in Brasília (`abr[].mu[].zon[].sec[].ha`). This is the counting curve.
- `docs/site/data/`: `frames.png` (5,571 towns x 681 minutes), `towns.json` (votes, peak,
  queue, half, p90, last), `totals.json`, `events.json` (16 beats). Reusable for the map screen.
- Zona numbers repeat across states; zona plus seção identifies an urna only with the state.

## Decisions (2026-10-29)

- D1 Reader: a voter, on WhatsApp, Instagram and in the press. pt-BR only.
- D2 Job: the reader feels part of something historical: amazed by the urna and the TSE, sure
  their vote counted, aware the country is big and still one thing.
- D3 Walk: scroll, one long page, about ten screens. No tap-through, no autoplay, no loop.
- D4 Spine: curiosities. Each screen is one true, checkable fact with a place and a minute.
  Milestones (1M, 25M, half) are asides. The first screen is one real log line, one vote.
- D5 Turn: after the awe screens, "onde você votou?" One field, the town. Optional: zona and
  seção, which opens the exact urna. A skip link is always visible.
- D6 Ownership without the minute: the page never knows when the reader voted. Town facts;
  section facts if typed (open, votes, close, bulletin arrival); one optional tap on an hour
  band, "mais ou menos que horas?", gives "por volta das 10h, N pessoas votaram com você em
  <town>", labelled approximate.
- D7 Mood: awe builds, pride lands. Quiet, no triumph.
- D8 Trust: show the paper trail, teach each term in plain words when it appears, link every
  number to its public source. The word "seguro" never appears.
- D9 Second page "Como funciona e como fizemos": one page, two parts. Part 1 the urna's day,
  term by term (zerésima = o recibo de zero votos, impresso antes do primeiro eleitor). Part 2
  sources, method, downloads. Every term in the walk links to its line.
- D10 End: the map runs to 19:06; the bulletins arrive 17:04 to 23:27 from real times; then
  the card with a share button and the link to the second page.
- D11 Look: new identity. Night ground, warm off-white type, one accent the colour of the
  urna's screen light, a text face with character (Google Fonts), big type. The v1 map is a
  reference, not a constraint; the light scale is explained before the map.
- D12 Placement: build at `docs/site/v2/`, judge there, then swap: v2 at `/`, v1 at
  `/explorar`. Town share links keep working.
- D13 Later page "Apuração": the reader's urna reaching the TSE, the curve loading, the
  count at 23:27 celebrated. Both names symmetric (same type, colour, ballot order) at the
  minute the result was defined. Celebrate the count, never the winner. Not in this plan.
- D14 Sound: later. Share reels: later; the card is the unit that connects readers.

## The ten screens (rough)

1. One vote: a log line, then the next, then the urna around it.
2. The first urna to open and the first vote of the country.
3. 08:00 all at once: 497,896 urnas, the peak 08:07, Acre at 06:00 local.
4. The differences: Marajó at 10:26, Alto Solimões 90% by 14:00, the Northeast's lunch dip.
5. The hard day: Belo Horizonte on battery, replaced urnas, the longest queue.
6. The turn: town, optional zona and seção, skip.
7. The map, lit, your town ringed. Runs the day once.
8. The close: 17:00, the STF at 17:15, the last vote 19:06.
9. The night: bulletins arriving 17:04 to 23:27, yours among them.
10. The card: "Você foi um dos 127.854.844." Share. Link to the second page.

## Tasks

### W1 data (`scripts/site_data.py`, output `docs/site/v2/data/`)
- `curiosidades.json`: the facts behind screens 1 to 5 and 8, each with place, minute (Brasília),
  value and the SQL that produced it.
- `horas.json`: votes per town per hour (for D6's hour tap), from `main.votos_minuto`.
- `secoes/<uf>/<zona>.json`: per-section facts (abertura, votos, encerramento, `ha`) so the
  page fetches one small file per zona.
- `chegada.json`: sections arrived per minute, national and per state, from `data/raw/cs/`.
  Check on a 1% sample of per-section `aux.json` that `ha` is the first arrival (risk R4 in P2's
  notes), and name which it is on the second page.
- Done when every number on the page has a row in one of these files.

### W2 page (`docs/site/v2/index.html`)
- The ten screens, scroll with sticky canvases, phone first, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured.
- Map screen reuses `frames.png` and `towns.json`; the ring and the hour tap are new.
- Copy in pt-BR, short sentences, no em-dashes, every term linked to `como.html#<term>`.
- Rough first: placeholder copy allowed, counting simulated and labelled if W1 is not ready.

### W3 second page (`docs/site/v2/como.html`)
- Part 1 the urna's day, one anchored paragraph per term. Part 2 sources (TSE URLs, this repo,
  the dataset), method, the 9 mismatched sections, the 1,352 sections without a log.

### W4 card
- Drawn on a canvas: town, urna if given, hour if tapped, "um dos 127.854.844". Web Share API
  with a file on phones, download on desktop. Per-town OG image later.

### W5 swap (after Robson's go on the rough build)
- v2 to `/`, v1 to `/explorar`, redirects for town share links, README updated.

## Rules for every task
- Work in a worktree on a branch from `main`; one PR per task; no merge without a go.
- Test server on port 8766 from the worktree. Port 8765 serves main; never stop it.
- Commits `type(scope): subject`, at most two body lines.
- No fraud claims, no side colours, no candidate names except per D13.

## Not in scope
- Any statement about who won, vote shares over time, or the result before D13's page.
- Round 1, video, sound, English.
